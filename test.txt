Physics Educational Sample Data
================================

Topic 1: Motion
---------------
Motion is the change in position of an object over time.
Speed is the distance traveled divided by time.
Velocity is speed with direction.
Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity.

Formula:
Speed = Distance / Time
Acceleration = (Final Velocity - Initial Velocity) / Time

Example:
If a car travels 100 meters in 10 seconds,
Speed = 100 / 10 = 10 m/s


Topic 2: Newton's Laws of Motion
--------------------------------

First Law:
An object remains at rest or in uniform motion unless acted upon by an external force.

Second Law:
Force equals mass times acceleration.
Formula: F = m × a

Third Law:
For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.


Topic 3: Energy
---------------

Energy is the ability to do work.

Kinetic Energy:
Energy of motion.
Formula: KE = 1/2 × m × v^2

Potential Energy:
Stored energy due to position.
Formula: PE = m × g × h

Where:
m = mass
v = velocity
g = gravity (9.8 m/s^2)
h = height


Topic 4: Electricity
--------------------

Electric current is the flow of electric charge.
Voltage is the electrical potential difference.
Resistance is the opposition to current flow.

Ohm's Law:
V = I × R

Where:
V = Voltage
I = Current
R = Resistance


Topic 5: Waves
--------------

A wave is a disturbance that transfers energy.
Wavelength is the distance between two wave peaks.
Frequency is the number of waves per second.

Wave Speed Formula:
Wave Speed = Frequency × Wavelength
